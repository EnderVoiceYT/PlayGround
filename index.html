HOW TO SURVIVE

THE LIGHT

Your flashlight is a weapon. The Hollow recoil from it and burn in its beam. Moths are drawn to it and drink the battery. The Gaunt does not care.

F toggle · B install cell · Batteries are finite.

THE DARK

Enemies see farther when you are lit, and hear you when you run. Switch the beam off to hide, but the dark is never empty.

Shift sprint · footsteps and gunfire carry.

THE GRID

Recover three ceramic fuses, restore the generator, and take the service elevator. Power wakes the whole building with you.

E interact · quest markers on the minimap.

THE RECORDERS

Three emergency recorders hold two charges each. Save sparingly. Death returns you to the last recorded checkpoint.

Tab pack · Esc pause · Notes can be reread.
